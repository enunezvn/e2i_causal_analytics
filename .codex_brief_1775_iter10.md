# Codex audit — iteration 10 — issue #1775, PR #1776 (head b723770b3)

## What this branch does

Grounds the causal-analysis clinical-context panel in the SCENARIO being analysed.
Before, the panel showed accurate indication/mechanism/RWE facts that were identical
regardless of which outcome the causal analysis estimated, and for COMMERCIAL levers
it shipped an honest refusal. Now it also returns `analysis_grounding`: verbatim FDA
label considerations selected by relevance to the analysed outcome, plus competitive
framing, plus a note tying them to the commercial question. Zero new upstream calls —
the openFDA label is already fetched for the indications fragment.

## What changed since iteration 9

Both findings accepted; both were larger than they looked.

1. HIGH — you were right that the merge persisted, and the cause was MY BACKSTOP.
   `_swallowed_a_boundary` is documented as defence in depth independent of the
   boundary pattern, and it located internal references with `_CANDIDATE` — the very
   pattern whose blind spots it covers. It now scans with a pattern of its own, so
   "; ", " and ", "/", "[5.1]" and "5.1.1" all drop instead of merging.
   Then the FIRST version of that scanner (any bracketed group with a digit,
   justified as "loose is safe, it can only drop") threw away a real alpelisib
   bullet: "Pediatric patients (2 to less than 18 years of age)" is an age range and
   "2" names the dosage section. It now requires the group to hold nothing but
   reference numbers and separators.
2. MEDIUM — a whole CONVENTION, not an edge case. Labels that write the sentence
   period AFTER the citation ("... thereafter ( 5.1 ).") parsed to NOTHING: 10 of 82
   live sections, every section of ivosidenib and atorvastatin, both of
   spironolactone's. A second bug sat behind it — under that convention the LAST
   bullet ends "( 5.2 )." so `after.strip()` saw content, demanded a capital, and
   dropped the final bullet of every such section.

Measured across 28 live labels / 82 sections: 232 -> 252 items, 20 GAINED, 0 LOST,
0 verbatim or adjacency violations.
