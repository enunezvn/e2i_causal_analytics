3. **MEDIUM — “Bernoulli residual is independent of the null tbin” is technically false; “conditionally mean-zero” is the correct statement.** The uniform coin is independent of the treatment stream, so \(E[Y-p\mid p,T]=0\). But \(Y-p\)’s variance is \(p(1-p)\), and \(p\) varies with covariates and co-exposures. On Remibrutinib, the residual variances are 0.138 versus 0.147 across null arms and the realized residual correlation with the tbin is +0.055. This does not invalidate the chance explanation. A heteroskedasticity-robust recalculation changes the reported residual z only from 3.28 to 3.25.

Conditional on the fixed seed-427 structural probabilities, the OLS coin-flip statistic is about 3.24 SD from its conditional mean—approximately 0.12% two-sided—not the quoted 1%.
